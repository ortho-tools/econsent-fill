Fill consent

Fills the KSUMC eConsent surgical and blood forms on your iPhone from one block your AI writes. You type only the MRN. Names, IDs, wording and Arabic are filled for you.

It never submits and never signs. You check, the patient and staff sign, you submit.

Set up once (5 minutes)

1

Allow scripts. iPhone Settings → Apps → Shortcuts → Advanced → Allow Running Scripts: on.

2

Install the Shortcut from the link you were sent → Add Shortcut. It asks for your defaults:
after my registrar: your usual registrar's short name from the staff list (e.g. tamer),
after my resident: the resident who is your second witness (e.g. hayfa).
New rotation? Shortcuts app → tap ⋯ on Fill consent → edit the first box.

3

Teach your AI. Make a project in your AI app and paste this prompt as its instructions:

  • ChatGPT: Projects → New project → ⋯ → Instructions
  • Claude: Projects → New project → Project instructions
  • Gemini: Gems → New Gem → Instructions

4

Put it near the top of the Share menu (optional). In Safari tap Share, scroll down → Edit Actions → tap ＋ next to Fill consent.

Each patient

"Fill" below always means: in Safari, tap Share → Fill consent.
At the station
  1. In your AI project: consultant, nurse, procedure. For example alshaygy raghad right TKR. Add high risk, long or short if needed. Tap copy on the block.
  2. Safari, surgical consent → Fill. Type the MRN in the box → Done. The page reloads with the patient: check the name.
  3. The blood consent opens in a new tab: Fill there too.
At the bedside
  1. Patient signs: tap Patient on each form, they sign each.
  2. Guardian or relative signs: on the surgical form tap Guardian/Relative, type the name (and relation), they sign, then Fill (it saves the signer). On the blood form tap the same signer yourself, then Fill (it adds the name). They sign.
Back at the station
  1. Different nurse? Fill on each form, then tap the right name in the yellow box under the witness.
  2. Nurse, second witness and you sign both → Submit both.

The staff list

Nurses, consultants, registrars and residents live in one shared Google Sheet (the link you were sent). New nurses add themselves: when Fill doesn't know the name, it asks for the full name (first and last) and adds it to the Sheet for everyone. Type - there for no nurse. You can also add one by hand: a new row with nurse, the first name in small letters, the full name. Everyone has it on their next Fill. The Sheet's "How to edit" tab has the details, and its version history undoes mistakes.

If something goes wrong

⚠️The message says what's wrong: no block copied, a different patient, a name not on the list. Nothing was changed when it stops.
❓Fill needs one answer (e.g. a new nurse's full name). Type it and tap Done.
🔄The page needed to reload first. When it has, Fill again.
Nothing happensSetup step 1, or you ran it from a page that isn't the consent form.
"No internet"It used its saved copy. Fine to carry on.
Fields stop fillingThe hospital changed the site. Contact the developer, with a screenshot.

Fallback: copy each part from your AI and paste it into the form by hand.

Patient privacy: never type the patient's name or MRN into your AI. The MRN goes only into the Shortcut's box on your phone.
